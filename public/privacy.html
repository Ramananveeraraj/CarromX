CarromX Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

CarromX ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a sports tournament management platform for organizing and participating in local carrom tournaments. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Your full name and phone number provided during OTP authentication to verify user identity.
  • Tournament Participation: Tournament registration history, board assignments, match scores, rankings, and referee verification logs.
  • Device & Connection Data: Network connection state to maintain real-time WebSocket match sync and push notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Authenticating organizers, referees, and players.
  • Generating tournament fixtures, brackets, and live scoreboards.
  • Delivering 10-minute pre-match notifications and dispute alerts.
  • Maintaining legitimate tournament match records and sports integrity.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Match fixtures and participant names are visible only to fellow participants and tournament organizers as required for tournament operations.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL), JWT token authentication, and secure database practices to protect your data from unauthorized access or modification.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request access to your personal data or ask for the deletion of your account and related records. To request account deletion or data inquiry, please contact our help desk at support@carromx.app.
